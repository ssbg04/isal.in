Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Core Privacy Guarantee: Isalin Multi-Converter runs 100% inside your web browser. When you convert images, videos, audio, PDFs, CSVs, or enter salary information, your files and data are never uploaded to any remote server.

1. Introduction

Isalin Multi-Converter ("Isalin", "we", "us", or "our"), accessible at https://isal.in, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains our zero-upload client-side architecture, what minimal operational data is collected, how advertising and analytics cookies function, and your rights under applicable privacy laws, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. The Zero-Upload Client-Side Architecture

Unlike traditional file conversion websites that upload your confidential files to remote servers, Isalin processes everything directly in your browser using modern Web APIs:

  • Image Processing: Handled via HTML5 Canvas API and WebAssembly without sending raw image buffers across the network.
  • Audio & Video: Transcoded locally inside browser sandboxes utilizing client-side Web Audio and video encoding workers.
  • PDF Generation: Handled client-side using pdf-lib and pdfjs-dist.
  • Financial & Personal Calculators: TRAIN law income tax, mortgage calculations, electricity splits, and college GWA are computed in memory on your device. Numbers never leave your browser tab.

3. Local Storage and Client-Side State

Isalin may store limited preferences locally on your device via browser localStorage:

  • mc_theme: Remembers your dark mode or light mode appearance preference.
  • mc_history: Stores your recent conversion history locally so you can revisit recent outputs. This history resides entirely on your device and can be cleared at any time with the "Clear History" button.

4. Analytics and Telemetry

We use Vercel Web Analytics to measure general site traffic and monitor system stability. Vercel Web Analytics:

  • Does not use tracking cookies or persistent device identifiers.
  • Does not collect or log personal data, IP addresses, or file contents.
  • Aggregates page views and performance metrics anonymously.

  • Third-party ad networks or ad servers may use cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their advertisements. Isalin does not control or have access to cookies used by third-party advertisers.
