Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are my files uploaded or stored on any server?

No. All file conversions, formatting, and mathematical computations happen locally inside your web browser. Your data never touches any remote server or backend database.

Can I use Isalin offline?

Yes. Isalin is engineered as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Once the site is loaded, all core converters and calculators remain completely functional without an active internet connection.

Is Isalin free?

Yes, all conversion tools and calculators are 100% free with no account creation or subscription required.
